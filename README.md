### How do I know which logo style fits my business?

Consider your industry norms and target audience—professional services typically use clean wordmarks or abstract symbols, while creative businesses can embrace illustrated icons and playful designs. Most AI logo generators ask about your industry and style preferences, then generate options matching those parameters, making it easier to see what resonates.

### Can I trademark a logo created by AI tools?

Yes, logos generated by AI tools are trademarkable as long as they're unique and you have commercial ownership rights. Premium packages from platforms like Looka include full commercial ownership, but verify your specific platform's terms before filing trademark applications. Some basic packages offer limited usage rights.

### What file formats do I need for different applications?

PNG files work for websites and digital use, especially with transparent backgrounds. Vector formats like SVG and EPS scale infinitely without losing quality, essential for print materials, signage, and large-format applications. Most comprehensive logo packages include multiple formats covering all use cases.
